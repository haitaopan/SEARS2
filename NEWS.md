# SEARS2 0.1.0

* Initial GitHub development release.
* Implements decision-specific evidence authority.
* Adds claim-specific activity-acceptability, activity-superiority, and tolerability-advantage posterior evidence.
* Adds the four-state final decision mapping: SELECT_ONE, RETAIN_SET, NO_ACCEPTABLE_DOSE, and DEFER.
* Includes frozen manuscript defaults and scenario definitions.
* Includes lightweight simulation helpers and regression tests.
